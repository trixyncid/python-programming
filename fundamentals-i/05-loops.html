Loops

Repeat work with for and while, place a loop inside a loop, and steer it with break, continue, and pass.

The Same Steps, Many Times

Use for when you know the collection. Use while when you know the condition that should keep the loop going.

  • range() counts for you
  • A while loop needs a change that can end it
  • break, continue, and pass steer one repetition
